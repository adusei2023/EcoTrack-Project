💡 Eco-Friendly Tips

🚲

Use Active Transport

Walk, bike, or use public transport to reduce your carbon footprint by up to 2.5 tons per year.

♻️

Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

Recycling and composting can save 1,200 lbs of CO2 annually per household.

🌱

Plant Trees

A single tree can absorb 48 lbs of CO2 per year. Plant native species in your area.

💡

Energy Efficient Appliances

Switch to LED bulbs and Energy Star appliances to cut energy use by 30-50%.

🥗

Eat Local & Seasonal

Reduce food miles and support local farmers. A plant-based meal can save 2.5kg of CO2.

💧

Conserve Water

Fix leaks and use water-efficient fixtures. Treating water requires energy and produces CO2.
